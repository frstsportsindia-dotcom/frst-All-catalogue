FRST SPORTS - SIMPLE DESIGN CATALOGUE

1) Open index.html in browser.
2) In index.html replace:
   WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "919999999999"
   with your real WhatsApp number.
3) Replace sample image URLs in the designs list with your own mockup images.
4) To add a design, copy one line like:
   {code:"CR-004", sport:"Cricket", image:"images/cr-004.jpg"},
5) For best speed, keep images around 1200px tall and compress to WebP/JPG.
6) Upload index.html + images folder to Netlify / Vercel / your hosting.

No database. No login. No backend. Very fast.
